Privacy Policy

How WiFi Accessories protects customer information and handles Google Business Profile data.

Effective October 29, 2026 · WiFi Accessories · Niagara Falls, Ontario
Who we areInformation we collectHow we use informationGoogle API & OAuthSharingStorage & retentionSecurityYour choicesPolicy updatesContact

Our approach: We use personal information only for purposes connected to our services and the integrations described here. Our Google Business Profile integration is authorized by a business owner or manager; ordinary website visitors do not need to sign in to Google to browse our website or read reviews.

01

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to WiFi Accessories, our website at wifiaccessories.ca, our repair-related online communications and the WiFi Accessories Reviews Integration used with our authorized Google Business Profile.

WiFi Accessories is a device repair, accessories and IT services business located at 7853 Butternut Blvd, Niagara Falls, ON L2H 0N3, Canada. For questions about this policy, contact wifiaccessories@gmail.com.

02

Information we collect

Information you provide

When you contact us or request a repair, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, device details, service request or repair description, appointment preferences, relevant location information for on-site services and any message you choose to share.

Technical information

Our website hosting and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device type, requested pages, timestamps and diagnostic logs. External content and communication services, when enabled, may process additional information under their own privacy policies.

Google Business Profile review information

Through our approved Google Business Profile API connection, we may receive our business's listing identifiers and review information such as review IDs, customer display names, available profile photographs, ratings, review comments, review dates, overall rating and total review count.

03

How we use information

  • To respond to questions and repair bookings, provide services and communicate about appointments or repairs.
  • To maintain records relevant to our services, investigate technical problems and help protect our website.
  • To monitor and manage customer feedback for our own verified Google Business Profile.
  • Where allowed by Google's applicable terms, to show appropriately attributed customer reviews and rating information on our business website.
  • To comply with applicable laws and legitimate business obligations.

We do not treat a repair inquiry as permission to send unrelated promotional messages.

04

Google Business Profile API and OAuth

The Google permission we request

Our Google OAuth application is named WiFi Accessories Reviews Integration. The business account owner or an authorized manager grants access using the Google permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage. This permission can provide broader Business Profile management access; our review integration is intended to use it to identify our authorized business account and location and retrieve review information for business feedback management.

How Google information is used and displayed

Our review carousel is designed to display selected five-star reviews from our Business Profile. If public display is permitted by Google, information visible to website visitors may include a reviewer's public display name, available Google profile photo, star rating, review text and approximate review date. Other ratings are not included in this showcase, but the overall Google rating and total review count reflect the complete profile, and visitors can follow our Google link to see the full set of reviews.

Public review display is subject to Google's applicable Business Profile API permissions and policies. We will not use the API for a public display that Google does not permit.

Google data we do not request

Customers visiting our website are not asked to grant Google permissions. This integration does not request website visitors' Gmail messages, Google Drive files, passwords, contact lists or private Google Account content.

How Google access is secured and revoked

Authorization is granted through Google's OAuth flow by the business account owner or an authorized manager. Access tokens are used for authenticated requests, and a refresh token may be held in private server-side configuration to renew access. OAuth client secrets and tokens are not intended to appear in public website code.

The Google Account holder can revoke this app's access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized API requests using that grant. For questions about deletion of any information retained by us, contact our privacy email below.

Google data commitment. We will handle Google API data according to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Business Profile API policies. We do not sell Google API information, use it for personalized advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.
05

When information is shared

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate our website, communicate with customers, manage bookings, provide technical support and run authorized Google API integrations. These providers may process information in Canada or other countries, subject to their services and applicable safeguards.

If an approved review display is active, the public review information described above may be shown to website visitors. We may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect legitimate rights. We do not sell customer information or Google API information to data brokers.

Links to services such as Google Maps, WhatsApp or other third-party websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Where third-party review widgets are active, those services may also process website usage information.

06

Storage, retention and deletion

We retain customer messages, repair and booking records only for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, business administration, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Information that is no longer needed is deleted or securely disposed of according to applicable requirements.

Google API review information may be processed temporarily to support our authorized integration. Any temporary caching must follow Google's rules, including applicable storage limitations; Google's Business Profile API policies limit permitted temporary stored content to no more than 30 calendar days. We do not intend to maintain a permanent independent database of Google review content.

OAuth credentials are kept separately from public website pages, restricted to authorized access, and revoked or removed when no longer needed. A Google review posted on Google's platform is controlled through Google; removing content from our website does not remove its original Google review.

07

Security practices

Our intended safeguards include encrypted HTTPS connections, Google OAuth permissions, access controls and private server-side storage of API credentials. We limit access to information to people and service providers who need it for legitimate purposes.

No website or system is completely risk-free. If you believe your information has been used improperly, contact us at the address below.

08

Your choices and privacy requests

  • You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we control, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • You can contact us about a review displayed on our site. To edit or remove a review you posted on Google, use Google's review management tools.
  • The authorized business account owner or manager can revoke OAuth access through the Google Account.
  • You can manage cookie and external-content settings using your browser controls.

We handle privacy questions under applicable Canadian privacy requirements, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where it applies.

09

Updates to this policy

We may revise this policy as our services, website or Google integration changes. A revised version will be published at this address with a new effective date. If we materially change the purpose for which we use Google user data, we will provide any disclosures and obtain any additional consent required before using the data for that new purpose.

Questions about privacy?

We are happy to explain how information is used or help with a privacy request. Please include Privacy Request in your email subject line.
